How Perfect Shuffle Works

1

Choose Your Layout

Clock Layout: 13 positions (Center 0, plus 1–12 like hours on a clock). Perfect for 52-card standard decks (exactly 4 cards per pile!).

3×3 Grid: 9 positions (1–9). Great for smaller tables or games with 60+ cards.

2

Lay Out Cards (Two Methods)

🎯 Counted Piles: Deal cards evenly face-down (e.g. 4 cards each for 52 cards on the Clock). The app tracks remaining cards and never calls an empty pile.

⚡ Casual Spread (Fast Setup): Drop clumps or casually spread cards without counting! If an announced spot is empty, simply take from the next clockwise pile.

Cards stay face down the entire time.

3

Pick as Dictated

The app calls out where to grab the next card (e.g. "Seven", "Center", "Twelve") with voice and high-contrast visuals. Pick the top card from that pile and stack it face-down onto your collected hand.

4

Hands-Free & Non-Destructive

Tap anywhere on screen (with a finger or knuckle) to advance, or turn on Auto-Pacer to pick in rhythm without touching your device. When all cards are collected, your deck is 100% truly randomized with zero card bending or riffle damage!
